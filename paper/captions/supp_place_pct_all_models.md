**Supplementary Figure 4. Task-stage decoding accuracy (A→B) across place
cells (%), all three models on shared axes.** Chance = 0.20 (KNN classifier
over 5 CS/US bins, not R²), shown as a dashed line. Same slice as
Supplementary Figure 3 (task-responsive cells = 40%, task weight held to the
two grid points nearest bioreal's still-provisional balance=0.5), extended to
arousal-dependent (ADM) so all three models share one plot instead of just
IM/PDM. Mean +/- 95% CI across 3 iterations per point; dashed marker line
marks bioreal's chosen 20%.

**ADM's curve is identical in both panels.** Unlike IM/PDM, arousal-dependent
has no task-weight parameter -- its slice is fixed at task cells carried over
A→B = 100% (matching IM/PDM's own fixed value in Supplementary Figure 1)
regardless of which task-weight panel it's drawn in. This is a real modeling
asymmetry (the model doesn't use that parameter at all), not a plotting
artifact. ADM sits on the chance line across the entire place-cell range,
consistent with Supplementary Figure 1's finding that it clears the
above-chance bar least often of the three models.
